Maripá

Maripá is a Brazilian municipality in the state of Paraná, located in the western region, about 600 km from Curitiba. Known as the "City of Orchids" and the state capital of Tractor Drag Racing, the city has just over 6,800 inhabitants and stands out for its strong agricultural and fish farming production.

  • City of Orchids: Thousands of orchids are cultivated and decorate the trees in the central streets and squares.
  • Tractor Drag Racing: Traditional annual city event that attracts visitors from all over the region.
  • Fish Farming: The municipality is one of the main references and powers in fish production in the state.

Agriculture & Livestock

The city stands out in the production of fish, soybeans, and corn.

Orchid Festival

It is the municipality's largest festival, taking place once a year and lasting 4 days, featuring orchid exhibitions, fish-based food (mainly tilapia), stands, amusement park rides, and performances/shows.

Downtown Neighborhood

Downtown Maripá is the heart of the city, that place where everything happens and where history seems alive in the streets. Back at the beginning of the 20th century, everything started around the old train station, created on October 10, 1972, which was the reason the neighborhood grew and became so important.

The downtown streets have a special feel, with old buildings that tell a bit about the lives of former residents, mixed with more modern constructions that show how the city has changed. In the middle of the neighborhood, there is the Central Square, with that charming gazebo and large trees that have seen a lot happen. The square is where people like to meet, chat, and enjoy events, especially on weekends during the market.

Downtown is also where the Municipal Library, the Arts Theater, and the Historical Museum are located. Lately, the neighborhood is undergoing nice changes, trying to become more sustainable and welcoming to everyone, which has brought improvements to the way of living there.

If you come to Maripá, you must take a walk around Downtown.

Municipal Development and Environmental Actions

The municipality of Maripá (PR) carries out environmental actions focused on sustainability, preservation of water resources, and ecological education. For example:

Protection and Recovery of Springs: The program focuses on recovering springs on local rural properties. This measure protects the water flow essential for local fish farming.

Strategic Partnerships: The Department of Agriculture and Environment works alongside environmental managers from Itaipu Binacional on sanitation, waste management, and conservation projects.

Environmental Education and Planting: The city promotes tree and orchid planting — connecting to Maripá's identity as the "City of Orchids" — as well as fingerling release and pedagogical activities with municipal students.

Selective Garbage Collection: The municipality has selective garbage collection in both urban and rural areas.

Sanitation Planning: Maripá has a structured Municipal Basic Sanitation Plan to guide the sustainable growth of urban and rural infrastructure.
